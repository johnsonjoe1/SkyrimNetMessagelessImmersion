# SkyrimNet Messageless Immersion 0.7.2

More useful inventory observations, better scene awareness, and fewer repeated thoughts. Changes since 0.7.1:

## New and expanded thoughts

- Added inventory thoughts for finding Waifu cards, replenishing lockpicks when supplies are low, reaching the last three lockpicks, and running out entirely.
- Added thoughts when obtaining your first or losing your last Chastity Key, Restraints Key, or Piercing Removal Tool. Fixed removal thoughts that previously never triggered.
- Expanded Battle Fuck responses for stripping stages and the start, progression, and end of the assault phase. Added handling for onlooker-arrival events when the mod broadcasts them.
- Restored SexLab Jail Rape comments about other prisoners, with clearer context and separate 45-second cooldowns for scene starts, orgasms, and endings. These comments are suppressed while the player is in a scene.
- Added thoughts about being hit by a Firebolt, with a 90-second cooldown, and about supported Devious Devices cow-bell effects.
- Added Bimbos of Skyrim responses to corruption cures from priests or vigilants, including cures that backfire. Spontaneous status complaints can now reflect high corruption levels above 60 and below 99.
- Improved prompts for Apropos 2 pain and healing, device-induced orgasms, Devious Followers debt, and nearby SLAC encounters. SLSF reputation thoughts now encourage references to relevant memories.

## Less repetition and better timing

- Added configurable SLSF fame-change thresholds and a shared cooldown. Defaults require a change of at least 5 points per category since the previous check and allow one fame thought every 300 seconds.
- Direct YPS thoughts now require at least 30 seconds of silence. Their prompts request the original wording as closely as possible and omit recent-thought history.
- Added a five-minute cooldown for swimming-related cleaning thoughts and suppressed water-exit comments after brief dips. Fixed magic-suppression effects incorrectly updating water-thought cooldowns.
- Added a shared three-minute cooldown for license and permit acquisition comments and bounty-resolution comments. Warnings about approaching guards remain immediate.
- AND nudity and flashing thoughts now have a ten-minute cooldown after starting a new game, shortened to at most 20 remaining seconds when RaceMenu closes.

## Fixes and response quality

- Loading or starting a game now clears recent thought history, resets SLSF baselines and its cooldown, and resets the tracked player scene state.
- Improved checks for player involvement in scene broadcasts. Nearby NPC encounters and slap notifications no longer incorrectly mark the player as being in a scene.
- Removed duplicate stage handlers and added support for an additional leg-cuff removal event name.
- Non-literal thought prompts now explicitly request first-person internal thoughts without physical actions, gestures, or stage directions.
- Reduced repetitive notification-test logging and debug HUD messages. Added clearer logging for inventory counts and skipped SLSF thoughts.

## New configuration options

In `SKSE/Plugins/SkyrimNetMessagelessImmersion.ini`, under `[Thoughts]`:

```ini
enableSLSFthoughts=1
SLSFthoughtThreshold=5
SLSFthoughtCooldown=300
```

- Set `enableSLSFthoughts=0` to disable SLSF thoughts.
- `SLSFthoughtThreshold` is the minimum change per category since the previous check. Set it to `0` to allow any change.
- `SLSFthoughtCooldown` is the shared cooldown in seconds. Set it to `0` to disable the cooldown.

Missing settings use the defaults above. Negative threshold or cooldown values also fall back to their defaults.
